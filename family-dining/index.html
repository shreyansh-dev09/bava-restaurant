A family restaurant with room for everyone

Bava’s is where families eat on weeknights and celebrate on weekends. Three ways to make the most of it.

All you can eat

Fixed-price spreads on select days with unlimited fried chicken, biryani, noodles and sides. Ideal for hungry groups and student crowds. Children under 8 eat at half price.

Happy-hour food

Reduced prices on starters and snacks during off-peak afternoon hours. The best-value time to try the whole fried chicken range with friends.

Private dining room

A separate, air-conditioned room for birthdays, office lunches and family gatherings. Seats up to 25. Book ahead and we’ll set a fixed menu with you.

Planning a celebration?

Tell us the date, headcount and occasion on WhatsApp. We confirm the room within a few hours and suggest a menu that fits your budget.

  • Birthday cake-cutting space and candles
  • Fixed menus from ₹300 per head
  • Kids’ portions of fried chicken and fried rice
  • Vegetarian and Jain options on request

Book the private room

Free to reserve. Minimum 10 guests.
