Questions people ask

What is Bava’s famous for?

Our fried chicken, first and foremost. After that, tandoori chicken, Chinese chicken dishes and Arabian grills like shawarma and Al Faham.

Is Bava’s family-friendly?

Yes. We’re a family restaurant with a private dining room for groups and celebrations.

How much does a meal cost?

Most meals fall between ₹200 and ₹400 per person. All-you-can-eat and happy-hour offers bring that down further.

Do you deliver?

Yes, on Swiggy and Zomato. Or call us for takeaway.

Can I book the private room?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp with your date and headcount, and we’ll plan the menu together.

Is there parking?

Street parking is available in front of the restaurant, and two-wheelers can park beside the entrance.
